A day it goes wrong

One ordinary failure at Ras Al Khair — and what it costs before anyone has done anything wrong
Rolling mill · one bearing
02:14
A bearing fails. The mill stops.
Normal wear. Nobody made a mistake here — machines fail.
02:40
Storeroom checked — not in stock.
400 metres away. It was never stocked. This is the failure.
03:10
Emergency order raised.
Standard lead time six weeks. Air freight instead.
→ 3–5× the planned price
+3 days
Part lands. Mill restarts.
Three days of flat-rolled output that will not be recovered.
→ Downtime dwarfs the part
Meanwhile · same storeroom · three aisles away
400 filter cartridges for a unit removed in 2019
Fully stocked. Perfectly counted. Correctly recorded in SAP. Not one of them will ever be issued.
SAR 300,000
It is not a distance problem — the store is 400 m from the mill. It is not a counting problem either; the stock records were right.
The money to buy that bearing had already been spent. It was sitting on a shelf, three aisles away, as something else.

What we are going to build

The seven capabilities in the Scope of Work — in order, in plain language, with the depth we propose for each
01Deep
Predict demand
How many of this part will we need in the next six months — from consumption history plus the maintenance plan. A planned shutdown is a known spike, not a surprise.
Buy at planned prices, not 3–5× emergency
02Deep
Find the dead money
Never-issued items · spares for scrapped equipment · the same part under three numbers · 400 held where 40 would do.
The largest one-time cash release
03Deep
Set the right levels
Reorder point and safety stock computed per item from its own demand shape, lead time and criticality — not a number typed in years ago.
Less capital frozen, same service level
04Solid
Catch what is wrong
Negative stock · consumption with no work order · missing units of measure · blank manufacturer part numbers · impossible lead times.
Data trust — everything else depends on it
05Solid
Move it, don't buy it
One storeroom holds 40 idle while another is about to buy five. Costed by distance — across the Ras Al Khair site, or 600 km from the mine.
Free inventory; avoids the purchase entirely
06Interactive
Price the trade-off
“What if we accept 95% service instead of 98%?” — cash released plotted against risk accepted, with the items each scenario touches.
Turns a stocking argument into a decision
07Deep
Ask it a question
“Which critical spares at the smelter are below reorder point?” — plain language, over PiLog MDRM and ERP data.
Adoption — no BI training needed
